Key Findings
💳
Billing & Payments is mis-understood
2,564
Most Billing tickets are actually "I paid, nothing arrived" — a dual billing + delivery signal. The Billing team correctly handles payment verification, then transfers ~19% to Logistics. These transfers are not errors — they're part of SOP 3.1.
📦
Delivery is the single biggest category
3,119
Our classifier puts Delivery & Shipping at 26.5% of all tickets (vs. 16.2% in original tags). Many "Billing" and "Other" tickets are actually delivery complaints. Resolution time averages 39.7 hrs — the 2nd slowest after Warranty.
🎯
Business goal: cut duplicate payments
₹14.1L
498 tickets tagged DUP-PAYMENT refunded ₹14.1L over 18 months (₹9.4L/yr). Auto-detecting double-charge events at checkout and proactively refunding before customers contact support could recover ~₹9L/year in support cost + goodwill.
🔄
1,299 unnecessary transfers
₹5,412
Each ticket transfer costs ~15 mins of agent time (₹31.25 at ₹20k/mo salary). 1,299 transfers = ₹40,594 lost over 18 months. Better upfront routing via this classifier could eliminate ~60% of those — saving ~₹16k/quarter.
⚡
Chat Frontline: fastest growing team
+73%
Chat Frontline volume grew from 608 (Jan–Jun 2025) to 1,052 (Jan–Jun 2026). It handles all tech support, product enquiries, and "Other" tickets. With 15 agents, the per-agent load is rising unsustainably.
⏱️
Warranty resolution: 131 hours avg
5.5 days
Warranty & Repair tickets take the longest to resolve at 131.3 hrs average. With only 6 agents in Escalations & Warranty, this is a capacity bottleneck. CSAT is likely dragged down here — worth investigating separately.
